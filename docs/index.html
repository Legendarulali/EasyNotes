Notes in your own handwriting.

EasyNotes is a notebook for stylus and touch: ruled and grid notebooks, endless whiteboards and the PDFs you write on. A Rust engine handles the ink, and your notes stay on your device.

For Windows and Linux 2-in-1s, Android tablets and iPad

Scroll for the tour
01 · Library

Your notes, on your device.

The library is a dark #1E1E1E desk of folders and notebooks, each card showing a live thumbnail of its first page. Every stroke saves locally right away. Sign in with Google only if you want a backup in your own Drive.

02 · Create

Ruled, grid, blank, or any PDF.

New makes a Ruled Notebook, a 5 mm Grid Notebook, an unbounded Blank Whiteboard or a folder. Import PDF turns lecture slides or a textbook chapter into a notebook you write straight onto.

03 · Canvas

Pages that keep coming.

Pages are 1000 × 1000 and stacked without gaps. Scroll or write near the end and the next one appears; blank pages you only scrolled past are dropped on close. Sticky notes go anywhere, and the optional magic wand snaps rough shapes into clean geometry.

04 · Pin to screen

Keep the formula in view.

Select a matrix, theorem or diagram and tap Pin. It becomes a sharp floating card that stays put while you scroll down the pages, switch tabs or go Home. Pins are never saved into the notebook or its exported PDF.

04 · Tools

One toolbar, docked where you like.

Pen, pencil and highlighter, two erasers, lasso and box selection, sticky notes and a 100-step undo, in a toolbar you can drag to any edge or corner. The top bar holds shape snap, finger drawing, split view and PDF export.

05 · Split screen

Two notebooks. One screen.

The textbook PDF on the left, your homework on the right, or one notebook twice. Each pane scrolls and zooms on its own, and your palm can rest on either.

    Everything in the box

    Every feature, at a glance.

    A Rust engine owns the document; Flutter draws it. Everything below works offline.

    Rust core engine

    A memory-safe engine (rust_engine) owns strokes, pages, selection, erasing and undo. It turns each stroke into variable-width ink with round joins, indexes everything in a QuadTree for fast hit tests, and recognises shapes in under 20 µs.

    • QuadTree
    • Pressure-sensitive ink
    • flutter_rust_bridge

    Workspaces & paper types

    Ruled Notebooks, 5 mm Grid Notebooks and unbounded Blank Whiteboards, organised in nested folders. Import any PDF straight into a notebook. Rename, move or delete from a right-click or long-press.

    • Ruled
    • Grid
    • Whiteboard
    • Folders
    • PDF import

    Smart pagination & status bar

    1000 × 1000 pages stacked with no gap. Scrolling or writing near the end appends the next page, and blank trailing pages you only scrolled past are dropped on close. The status bar shows zoom, position and Page X / Y. Tap it, or press Ctrl+0, to reset to 100 %.

    Precision inking, touch fallback & two erasers

    The first stylus contact turns on palm rejection: fingers pan and zoom. One tap in the top bar switches to finger drawing for when the stylus battery runs out. Pen, pencil and highlighter; 8 swatches plus any hex colour; a thickness slider. The Stroke eraser removes whole strokes, and the Classic eraser cuts them exactly where it passes, keeping sharp corners.

    9-shape instant geometry snap

    Turn on the magic wand (it is off by default) and rough shapes snap on pen-up: lines, circles, ellipses, triangles, rectangles and squares, trapezoids, 5- to 8-sided polygons and five-pointed stars. Letters written at text size stay ink.

    • 3–16 µs per stroke
    • Off by default

    Pin-to-screen reference cards

    Select a formula or diagram and tap Pin. EasyNotes renders it on its page background at up to 3× screen resolution, into a floating card you can drag and resize (aspect ratio locked), switch between 100 % and 75 % opacity, or close. Cards stay on screen while you scroll, switch tabs and go Home, and never end up in the notebook or its PDF.

    50/50 split screen & tabs

    Two panes side by side with a draggable divider (Ctrl+\): two notebooks, or one notebook twice. Each pane scrolls and zooms on its own, and palm rejection covers both. Tabs switch instantly with Ctrl+Tab, and the clipboard is shared, so you can copy in one notebook and paste in another.

    Full undo / redo & vector PDF export

    Every edit is one step: strokes, eraser drags, moves, pastes, sticky notes. 100 steps per notebook (about 64 MB at most), with Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. Export to a vector PDF that embeds imported pages as vector content, so their text stays selectable.

    Local-first + Google Drive backup

    Saving is local and instant, and no account is needed. If you sign in with Google, the app backs up to My Drive › EasyNotes/ every 3 minutes, when you close a tab, and before the app closes. It uses only the drive.file scope, so it can see only files it created. On Windows the session is encrypted with DPAPI.

    • drive.file only
    • Incremental
    • DPAPI

    Cross-platform architecture

    One Rust engine and one Flutter app, for Windows and Linux 2-in-1 laptops, Android tablets and iPad. The engine and the UI talk through flutter_rust_bridge.

    • Windows
    • Linux
    • Android
    • iPadOS
